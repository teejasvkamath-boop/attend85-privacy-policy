Attend85 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Attend85, developed by VISSAN Technologies, handles information when you use the Attend85 mobile application.

We respect your privacy and have designed Attend85 so that your attendance information is stored locally on your device.

2. Information Stored by Attend85

Attend85 may store the following information on your device:

  • Subject names
  • Attendance counts
  • Attendance records and dates
  • Attendance preferences and minimum attendance settings
  • App appearance and other settings you choose

3. How Your Information Is Stored

The information described above is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage/database functionality.

Attend85 does not currently require you to create an account or sign in.

4. Information We Collect

VISSAN Technologies does not currently collect or receive your attendance information on its own servers.

Attend85 does not currently use an account system, cloud synchronization, advertising services, or analytics services to transmit your attendance data to us.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored attendance information with third parties because Attend85 does not currently transmit this information to VISSAN Technologies or third-party servers.

6. Advertising

Attend85 does not currently contain advertisements and does not currently use advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Attend85 remains on your device until you delete it using available app functionality, reset the app data, or remove the application and its associated app data from your device.

Because Attend85 does not currently maintain an account or store attendance information on VISSAN Technologies' servers, there is currently no server-side attendance account to request deletion from.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to design Attend85 to keep attendance information within the user's device. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your device secure and use the security features provided by your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Attend85 is designed primarily for college students and is not specifically directed toward children.

Attend85 does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Attend85's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Attendance Disclaimer

Attend85 is an attendance calculation and planning tool. Attendance requirements and eligibility rules may differ between colleges, universities, courses, and institutions. Users should verify attendance requirements with their institution. VISSAN Technologies does not guarantee that calculations made using Attend85 will satisfy any particular institution's attendance policy.
